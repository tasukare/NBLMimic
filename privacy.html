Privacy Policy

Application: Research Lab (NBLMimic) • Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Architecture: Zero-Tracker / Client-Side

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

Research Lab (NBLMimic) is a privacy-first, self-hosted research and knowledge workspace inspired by Google NotebookLM. Our software is engineered around client-side cryptography, local storage primitives, and user-owned cloud services.

We believe that your personal notes, research artifacts, academic inquiries, and cloud files belong exclusively to you. The application operates directly in your browser and communicates with Google APIs using your explicit authorization. We run no centralized tracking servers, collect no telemetry, and do not profile or monetize your personal information.

2. Information We Access and Collect

When you choose to connect your Google account to Research Lab, the application requests access to specific Google services via Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0). We access only the permissions strictly required to execute user-initiated actions:

  • Google Account Identity: When authenticating via Google Identity Services, we receive basic profile attributes (name, email address, and profile picture avatar) strictly to display your active identity in the workspace header.
  • Google Drive Read Access (drive.readonly): Allows you to browse, search, and import your own documents (PDFs, Google Docs, text files) into your active research session as reference sources.
  • Google Drive App File Creation (drive.file): Allows the application to save, synchronize, and update your research notes, lab archives, and generated notebooks directly in your personal Google Drive storage.

3. How We Use Google User Data

Information accessed from Google APIs is processed exclusively within your local browser session for the following explicit purposes:

  • Parsing document text to provide contextual grounding for research inquiries and multi-source summaries.
  • Writing and maintaining your personal research notebook backups in your designated Google Drive folder.
  • Maintaining your authenticated session across browser reloads without requiring centralized login credentials.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your Google user data to data brokers, ad exchanges, or external commercial databases.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Google Limited Use Disclosure
Research Lab (NBLMimic)'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use access to user data to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the user interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to any third party, unless necessary to provide or improve features in accordance with your consent or as required by law.
  • We never use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your private user data unless you have given explicit consent for troubleshooting, it is necessary for security reasons, or to comply with applicable law.

5. Data Storage, Cryptography, and Security

NBLMimic implements strict hardware-bound and client-side encryption standards:

  • Client-Side Hardware Vaults: Sensitive credentials, encryption salts, and tokens are protected using the native Web Crypto API (AES-GCM-256) with non-extractable keys bound to your browser session.
  • Zero Central Database: Research Lab operates without an application database server. Your research materials exist only in your browser's local sandbox (IndexedDB / localStorage) and in your personal Google Drive account.
  • Secure Direct Transports: All network exchanges with Google APIs occur over TLS/HTTPS encrypted connections directly between your client browser and Google's authenticated endpoints (https://www.googleapis.com).

6. Artificial Intelligence & Model Processing

When you submit an analytical prompt or query your research sources, the relevant document excerpts and your prompt are transmitted directly to the Google Gemini API (via your configured API Key or local loopback proxy) to generate responses.

Your data is governed by the Google Cloud Gemini Terms of Service. Under standard API usage, data passed via Google API endpoints is not used to train or refine foundation models without authorization.

7. User Control, Data Deletion, and Revocation

You retain total authority over your access credentials and stored research at all times:

  • In-App Disconnection: Clicking Disconnect Google Drive or signing out instantly purges all OAuth tokens, active session headers, and cached user profile data from your browser memory and cryptographic vault.
  • Google Account Revocation: You can permanently revoke NBLMimic's access to your Google account at any time through Google's central security console: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Drive File Management: All files created by Research Lab in your Google Drive remain completely under your ownership and can be downloaded, exported, or deleted directly through the standard Google Drive web interface.
